## ActorDefinition: Child (Experimental) 

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Official URL*:http://smart.who.int/immunizations/ActorDefinition/IMMZ.Persona.Child | *Version*:0.2.0 |

A client who is 15 years of age or younger and who intends to receive immunization services from the targeted health worker personas. 
**Alternate names**: Infant, baby, client. 
A related generic persona of the WHO Digital Adaptation Kit for Immunizations.
