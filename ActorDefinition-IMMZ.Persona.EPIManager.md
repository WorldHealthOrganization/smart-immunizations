## ActorDefinition: EPI Manager (Experimental) 

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Official URL*:http://smart.who.int/immunizations/ActorDefinition/IMMZ.Persona.EPIManager | *Version*:0.2.0 |
| Draft as of 2026-11-19 | *Computable Name*:EPIManager |

 
Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI) managers develop annual and multi-annual plans, immunization communication and mobilization, management of cold chain and vaccine logistics, monitoring, supervision and evaluation of immunization services, and coordination of EPI activities at the national and subnational levels. 
**Alternate names**: Programme manager. 
**ISCO-08**: 1342 Manager, health service. 
A targeted generic persona of the WHO Digital Adaptation Kit for Immunizations.
